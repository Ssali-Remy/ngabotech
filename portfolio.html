Networking Illustrative Example

Office Network Overhaul

Challenge

Frequent Wi-Fi dead zones and a single point of failure at the router were interrupting daily work.

Solution

A full network redesign with managed switches, segmented VLANs and ongoing monitoring.

Outcome

Reliable coverage across the building and no more single point of failure.

Cloud & Data Illustrative Example

Cloud Migration for a Growing Retailer

Challenge

Data spread across local drives with no reliable backup or remote access.

Solution

Migrated file storage and backups to the cloud with automated, scheduled recovery.

Outcome

Staff can work from anywhere, and backups run without anyone remembering to trigger them.

Website & Digital Illustrative Example

Corporate Website Relaunch

Challenge

An outdated website that looked unprofessional and was hard for the team to update.

Solution

A redesigned, mobile-friendly website with a simple content management workflow.

Outcome

A site the team can actually keep up to date, without calling a developer for every change.

Specialized Systems Illustrative Example

CCTV & Access-Control Rollout

Challenge

No visibility into who entered the office after hours, and no camera coverage of entry points.

Solution

Installed CCTV covering all entry points and access-control on key doors.

Outcome

Clear visibility and an audit trail of who accessed the premises, and when.

IT Infrastructure Illustrative Example

Structured Cabling Installation

Challenge

An office running on a tangle of unlabeled cables and ad-hoc extensions.

Solution

A structured cabling installation with proper patch panels, testing and labeling.

Outcome

A network that's easy to troubleshoot and simple to extend as the office grows.

IT Support Illustrative Example

24/7 Helpdesk Rollout

Challenge

Staff waiting hours or days for IT issues to get resolved, with no clear process for requesting help.

Solution

A ticketing-based helpdesk with defined response times and an on-call escalation path.

Outcome

Issues get acknowledged fast, and staff know exactly where to turn.
