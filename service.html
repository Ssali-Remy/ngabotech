Services by Pillar

Three clear areas of expertise, working together to keep your business supported, secure and ready to grow.

IT Support

Responsive, practical help that keeps your people and devices working every day.

  • IT Helpdesk & Outsourcing — Helpdesk, remote and on-site support, new-hire setup and ongoing IT management.
  • Repairs & Maintenance — Troubleshooting, repair and preventive care for computers, printers and business equipment.
  • IT Asset Management — Tracking and maintaining computers, software and network equipment.
  • IT Procurement — Sourcing hardware and software suited to your needs and budget.

IT Infrastructure

Secure, dependable technology foundations built to support your business.

  • Infrastructure & Hardware — Computer, server and printer installation, structured cabling and equipment setup.
  • Networks & Wi-Fi — LAN/WAN setup, Wi-Fi optimization, VPNs, firewalls and network monitoring.
  • Cybersecurity — Security assessments, vulnerability scanning, phishing simulations and incident response.
  • Cloud & Data — Cloud migration, automated backups, disaster recovery and SaaS management.
  • Server Management — Installation, monitoring and maintenance of physical and cloud servers.
  • Backup & Disaster Recovery — Automated backups and recovery that minimize downtime and data loss.
  • CCTV Systems — Installation, configuration and maintenance of surveillance systems.
  • Biometric Systems — Fingerprint and facial-recognition identification systems.
  • Access-Control Systems — Managing who can access specific areas of your premises, and when.
  • Attendance Systems — Automated employee attendance and time-tracking solutions.
  • IT Policy & Compliance — Practical IT policies and controls for security and accountability.

IT Solutions

Digital tools and expert guidance that make work simpler and help your business grow.

  • Software & Systems — Operating systems, Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace, patch management and data migration.
  • Website & Digital Services — Website design, hosting, domains, e-commerce and ongoing maintenance.
  • Business Systems & Automation — CRM/ERP, document management, API integration and workflow automation.
  • IT Consulting — IT audits, infrastructure assessments, budgeting and business continuity planning.
  • IT Audits & Assessments — Reviewing your technology environment for risks and inefficiencies.
  • Digital Transformation — Replacing manual processes with modern, automated digital workflows.
  • Email & Collaboration — Professional email, shared calendars, file storage and collaboration.
  • VoIP & Office Telephone — Reliable, cost-effective business calling systems.
  • Meeting-Room Technology — Displays, video conferencing, audio and presentation setup.
  • POS Systems — Point-of-sale installation and support for sales and inventory.
  • Database Solutions — Design, setup and maintenance of secure, organized business databases.

Security & Compliance

Every engagement is built with data protection, access control and auditability as foundations, not afterthoughts.

Our approach: we handle client data in line with regional data protection regulations (such as Kenya's Data Protection Act and Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act) and take GDPR-aligned practices as our baseline for any engagement involving international data. Formal data handling terms are agreed with every retainer client before work begins.

Secure by Design

Security is built into every network, system and account setup from the start — not bolted on afterward.

Role-Based Access

Granular access permissions so staff and vendors only see the systems and data relevant to their role.

Backups & Encryption

Automated backups and encryption in transit and at rest, sized to your data and recovery needs.

Monitoring & Response

Ongoing monitoring and a documented incident response process so issues are caught early and handled fast.
