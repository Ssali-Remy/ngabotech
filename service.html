Security & Compliance

Every engagement is built with data protection, access control and auditability as foundations, not afterthoughts.

Our approach: we handle client data in line with regional data protection regulations (such as Kenya's Data Protection Act and Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act) and take GDPR-aligned practices as our baseline for any engagement involving international data. Formal data handling terms are agreed with every retainer client before work begins.

Secure by Design

Security is built into every network, system and account setup from the start — not bolted on afterward.

Role-Based Access

Granular access permissions so staff and vendors only see the systems and data relevant to their role.

Backups & Encryption

Automated backups and encryption in transit and at rest, sized to your data and recovery needs.

Monitoring & Response

Ongoing monitoring and a documented incident response process so issues are caught early and handled fast.
