Welcome to ngabotech

We are ngabotech — an outsourced IT department for businesses that would rather run on technology than manage it themselves. One contract, one team, one point of contact for everything IT.

Our Story

Growing businesses kept hiring IT help one problem at a time — a network technician here, a website freelancer there — with no one owning the whole picture. ngabotech exists to be that single, accountable IT partner instead: the outsourced department that sees the whole system, not just the ticket in front of it.
