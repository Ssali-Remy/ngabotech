Office Network Overhaul

A growing office had outgrown its network: dead Wi-Fi spots, a single point of failure at the router, and no segmentation between staff and guest traffic. Day-to-day work was interrupted by dropped connections and slow file transfers between departments.

What a project like this involves

Work typically starts with a full site survey to map coverage gaps and traffic patterns, followed by structured cabling where it's missing, and a redesign around managed switches and access points sized to the space. VLANs separate staff, guest and security traffic, and a site-to-site VPN links any additional locations. Once the physical network is stable, we layer in monitoring so problems get caught before anyone notices them.

The result: consistent Wi-Fi across the building, no more single point of failure, and a network that's documented and monitored instead of held together by memory. This is the kind of outcome we aim for on every infrastructure project — get in touch if this sounds like what your office needs.
